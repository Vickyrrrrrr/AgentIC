Design a SPI master controller. Mode 0 (CPOL=0 CPHA=0). 8-bit transfers. Inputs: clk, rst_n, spi_start, mosi_data (8-bit), miso. Outputs: sclk, mosi, cs_n, miso_data (8-bit), spi_done. When spi_start pulses high, assert cs_n low, clock out mosi_data MSB first on MOSI, sample MISO on rising SCLK edge, deassert cs_n after 8 bits, pulse spi_done for 1 cycle. SCLK is clk divided by 4. Module name is spi_master.
