Design a UART transmitter with configurable baud rate. Parameters: 9600 baud, 8 data bits, 1 stop bit, no parity. Inputs: clk, rst_n, tx_start, tx_data (8 bits). Outputs: tx (serial line), tx_busy. When tx_start pulses high the module serializes tx_data and transmits it LSB first with one start bit (low) and one stop bit (high). tx_busy stays high during transmission. Module name is uart_tx.
